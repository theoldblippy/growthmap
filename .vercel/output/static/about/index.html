I'm an operator, not a theorist.

A decade inside fast-growing companies, from employee #3 to 100+, usually the person leaders called when things started breaking.

How I got here

I started in project management and grew into operations. Along the way I learned to build what's called an operating model. It's basically a project plan, but for a business: how you get customers, what it takes to serve them, and the rhythm that keeps it all running.

I love operations and making the thing work, mainly because I hate waste. And after a decade of it, the same pattern kept showing up everywhere I looked:

X Founders making every decision themselves because nobody else had the context.
X Teams working hard but not on the same thing.
X Revenue unpredictable because nobody understood how the business actually created value.
X Owners carrying the anxiety of the entire company on their shoulders.

Every one of those companies thought it had a revenue problem. Almost every one had an operating problem wearing a revenue costume. Growth Map exists to find that problem, and then fix it with you.
